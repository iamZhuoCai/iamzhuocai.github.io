About Me

I am a PhD student at the University of Technology Sydney, advised by Dr. Shoujin Wang. I build generative recommendation systems — models that recommend by generating item identifiers directly, rather than scoring a fixed catalogue.

My work centres on discrete flow matching and diffusion for sequence generation, semantic-ID tokenization, efficient single-step inference, and credibility-aware, responsible recommendation.

  • Generative recommendation & semantic IDs
  • Discrete flow matching & diffusion
  • One-step / efficient generation
  • Credibility-aware & responsible recsys
  • NLP & information retrieval
